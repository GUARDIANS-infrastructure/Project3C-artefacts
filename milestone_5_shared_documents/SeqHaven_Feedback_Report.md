# SeqHaven User Feedback Report

## Background

QIMR Berghofer’s main goal for GUARDIANS Phase 1 was to establish a local system to store and share its genomic data. This data commons, called **SeqHaven**, was designed to enable data to be transferred efficiently to approved users in Australia and overseas in a controlled and secure manner.

A pilot version of the SeqHaven was demonstrated in January 2026 for Milestone 4 deliverables. Since then, it has been used on three occasions to share data with organisations in Australia, Sweden, and the United States of America. The datasets shared included the Australian ICGC Melanoma

With the pilot system in place, the next phase of work focuses on improving SeqHaven based on real user experience and feedback.

## How Feedback Was Collected

Feedback on the SeqHaven was gathered from two sources. First, a demonstration was provided to GUARDIANS’ partner organisations, allowing for discussion questions and suggestions about the platform's structure and uses. Second, a short survey was sent to data recipients (referred to as “users”) who had already received data using the system. Two of three survey responses were received.

## Feedback from Demonstration
Discussions during partner demonstrations identified some technical issues that will need to be addressed. One key issue was how to store and manage records that describe the contents of shared datasets. Partners also raised questions about how different data description formats would be supported.

## What Users Told Us

Overall, user survey feedback was positive. Participants highlighted the speed and reliability of data transfers using the SeqHaven. They also reported that the data transfer platform was easy to navigate and straightforward to use.

The main challenge reported related to users identifying their own organisational resources to receive the data transfers via Globus. In particular, users sometimes needed support from their local IT teams to identify suitable transfer options.

The availability of the SeqHaven support contact, who helped explain the available options and guide decision-making, was highlighted as a positive.

## Suggested Improvements

Users suggested that additional guidance materials would be helpful. In particular, they requested clear explanations of how data encryption and decryption works, as well as practical guides that show each step of the data transfer process using examples and screenshots.

## Next Steps

The QIMR Berghofer team has started developing step-by-step support materials to help data recipients use the SeqHaven more easily. These resources will be delivered as part of the next project milestone, due in November 2026.
In GUARDIANS Phase 2, there are plans to improve how dataset records are stored and managed. These changes aim to make the system more robust and easier to use as adoption increases.
